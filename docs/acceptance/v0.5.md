# Acceptance: v0.5

Scope: the 9 new icons of v0.5 (connectors, stations, a vehicle and an energy icon), to be listed in the 0.5.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and in the icon tables of the [README](../../README.md#icons).

## Delegation

On 2026-11-03 the owner (Landolsi Digital) approved the icon list for v0.5 and delegated the design decisions to the maintainer. The builder checked the spec on 2026-11-03. Icons are not approved by the owner one by one; instead:

1. The icons are drawn in three batches: the connector batch (`plug-gbt-dc`, `plug-mcs`), the station batch (`charging-availability`, `station-uptime`, `restricted-access`) and the mixed batch (`community-verified`, `charging-socket`, `motorcycle-electric`, `power-outage`).
2. An independent reviewer checks each batch with screenshots (`npm run snap`) at 16/20/24 px, light and dark.
3. After the reviewer's findings are resolved, the maintainer approves the batch: `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date of that approval. `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

All v0.5 icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.5.0"`, and `approvedBy: null`, `approvedOn: null` until their batch is approved.
