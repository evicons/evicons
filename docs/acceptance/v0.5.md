# Acceptance: v0.5

Scope: the 9 new icons of v0.5 (connectors, stations, a vehicle and an energy icon), to be listed in the 0.5.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and in the icon tables of the [README](../../README.md#icons).

## Delegation

On 2026-11-03 the owner (Landolsi Digital) approved the icon list for v0.5 and delegated the design decisions to the maintainer. The builder checked the spec on 2026-11-03. Icons are not approved by the owner one by one; instead:

1. The icons are drawn in three batches: the connector batch (`plug-gbt-dc`, `plug-mcs`), the station batch (`charging-availability`, `station-uptime`, `restricted-access`) and the mixed batch (`community-verified`, `charging-socket`, `motorcycle-electric`, `power-outage`).
2. An independent reviewer checks each batch with screenshots (`npm run snap`) at 16/20/24 px, light and dark.
3. After the reviewer's findings are resolved, the maintainer approves the batch: `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date of that approval. `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

All v0.5 icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.5.0"`, and `approvedBy: null`, `approvedOn: null` until their batch is approved.

## Lucide construction compared

For each motif below the Lucide icon is looked at as a visual reference only and the EVicons drawing is checked for a different construction (guide section 3, `tests/no-lucide.test.ts`).

| Batch | Icon | Lucide icon compared | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Station batch | `restricted-access` | `Lock` | Compared with lucide-static 1.60.0. Same generic padlock build-up (one body rect plus one shackle path), different proportions: body 10 × 10 (rx 1.5) with a shackle of radius 3 rising 5.5 above the body (55 % of the body height), against Lucide's 18 × 11 body (rx 2) with a shackle of radius 5 rising 9 (82 %); extra elements: the keyhole dot and the small bolt beside the body. No path shared (`tests/no-lucide.test.ts`). |
| Station batch | `station-uptime` | `ChartColumn`, `BarChart`, `Barcode` | Compared with lucide-static 1.60.0. `Barcode`: five equal free vertical lines (y 5–19, x 3/8/12/17/21); the first draft (five equal bars y 11–21.5) rebuilt that construction with x shifts of at most 0.75 and read as "barcode + bolt", so bar 4 was shortened to y 17–21.5 (a broken status strip: four days up, one day down) and the bolt sits above. `BarChart`: three free bars of rising height (6, 12, 18) standing on a common bottom at y 21, no axis, read as a growing quantity. `ChartColumn`: an L axis with a rounded corner and three bars of different heights inside it. EVicons: five bars at a fixed 4.25 pitch, four equal and one short in the second-to-last position, no axis or baseline, plus the small bolt; it reads as a status timeline with one outage, not as a quantity chart or a barcode. No path shared (`tests/no-lucide.test.ts`). |
