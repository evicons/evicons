## OCPI mapping

The values come from the Locations module of OCPI, `mod_locations.asciidoc` in github.com/ocpi/ocpi at tag `2.2.1` and at tag `v2.3.0`, both retrieved 2026-11-04. Every enum of a mapped field is copied into `tools/lib/ocpi.ts` with the first of the two versions that defines each value; the enums were compared between the two tags: 2.3.0 adds `MCS` and `SAE_J3400` to ConnectorType, `EMPLOYEES`, `TAXIS` and `TENANTS` to ParkingRestriction, and the new field `Connector.capabilities` (ConnectorCapability: `ISO_15118_2_PLUG_AND_CHARGE`, `ISO_15118_20_PLUG_AND_CHARGE`); Status, Capability, PowerType, ConnectorFormat, Facility, ParkingType and EnergySourceCategory are the same in both. 32 icons carry 47 values (41 from 2.2.1, 6 only from 2.3.0). `npm run lint:icons` checks the syntax, that the field and value exist in `tools/lib/ocpi.ts`, and that no value is mapped to two icons; `tests/build-icons.test.ts` checks `ocpi.json` against `icons.json`, and `tests/readme.test.ts` checks the tables in the README and in the docs page (with the OCPI version of each value) and runs the docs example.

Rulings (a value is mapped only when the icon means what the spec says):

- `EVSE.status`: `AVAILABLE`, `CHARGING` ("in use"), `RESERVED`, `OUTOFORDER` ("broken or defect") and `INOPERATIVE` ("not yet active or temporarily not available, but not broken") map to the six `status-*` icons by their meaning texts; `UNKNOWN` to `status-unknown`. Not mapped: `BLOCKED` (a physical barrier, no icon), `PLANNED`, `REMOVED`. The markers do not carry the values (one icon per value); the docs tell apps to swap `status-` for `marker-` on a map.
- `Connector.standard`: Tesla's `TESLA_S` and `TESLA_R` are not mapped to `plug-nacs`; NACS is `SAE_J3400` (2.3.0). `GBT_AC` is `plug-type-2` (same face, as `socket:gb_ac`). `DOMESTIC_F` (CEE 7/4, Schuko) is `plug-schuko`; `DOMESTIC_E` (French pin earth) is a different face and not mapped.
- `EVSE.capabilities`: all five payment terminal values are `card-terminal`, because the spec defines each as "EVSE has a payment terminal that ..."; `RFID_READER` is `charging-card`. Plug & Charge is only a `Connector.capabilities` value of 2.3.0.
- `Location.facilities`: `FUEL_STATION`, `MALL` and `SUPERMARKET`, `HOTEL` and `RESTAURANT` (`destination-charging`, whose meaning names hotels and restaurants; `CAFE` is not mapped). `PARKING_LOT` is not mapped, as a facility or as a `parking_type`: a car park is not a charging bay. `parking-charging` takes `EVSE.parking_restrictions=EV_ONLY` ("reserved parking spot for electric vehicles").
- `EVSE.parking_restrictions`: `CUSTOMERS` (2.2.1), `EMPLOYEES` and `TENANTS` (2.3.0) are `restricted-access`; `MOTORCYCLES` is `motorcycle-electric` (like OSM `motorcycle=yes`). Not mapped: `DISABLED` (a bay reserved for disabled people does not say that the charger is usable from a wheelchair, which is what `accessible-charging` means), `PLUGGED`, `TAXIS`.
- `Location.energy_mix`: `is_green_energy=true` (100 % renewable) is `green-power`. `energy_sources.source=SOLAR` is not mapped (the field stays in `tools/lib/ocpi.ts`): an entry gives the share of solar power in the mix, and any share does not mean charging with solar power, which is what `solar-charging` means; the docs tell apps to derive `solar-charging` from `percentage` (for example ≥ 50 %). The nested list would be written `Location.energy_mix.energy_sources.source`.
- The German charging point register of the Bundesnetzagentur and the AFIR feeds use their own field names; no mapping is shipped (docs, "Registers").

## OpenStreetMap tags

Checked on the OSM wiki on 2026-11-04: Key:wheelchair (`wheelchair=yes`: "Wheelchairs have full unrestricted access", status approved) maps to `accessible-charging`; Key:support (`support=street_lamp`: "The feature is supported by a street_lamp", status de facto) maps to `streetlight-charging`. `wheelchair=limited` and `wheelchair=no` are not mapped. The JOSM style uses only the `socket:*` tags and does not change.
