# Acceptance: v0.6

Scope: the OpenStreetMap metadata (`osm` field, `@evicons/svg/osm.json`) and the 2 new connector icons of v0.6, `plug-cee-blue` and `plug-cee-red`, listed in the 0.6.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and in the icon tables of the [README](../../README.md#icons).

## Delegation

Since 2026-11-01 the owner (Landolsi Digital) delegates the design decisions to the maintainer. The scope of v0.6 (OpenStreetMap mapping, CEE blue and CEE red, the docs section) was set by the maintainer on 2026-11-03, with one ruling made before drawing: GB/T AC gets no icon of its own, because `plug-type-2`'s meaning already states that the GB/T 20234.2 AC inlet has the same face; it is mapped through `socket:gb_ac` on `plug-type-2`. On approval `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date; `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person. Both icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.6.0"`.

## OpenStreetMap mapping

The tags come from the OSM wiki pages Key:socket and Tag:amenity=charging_station, retrieved 2026-11-03. 26 icons carry 33 tags; `npm run lint:icons` checks the tag syntax and that no tag is mapped to two icons; `tests/build-icons.test.ts` checks `osm.json` against `icons.json`, and `tests/readme.test.ts` checks the tables in the README and in the docs section against `osm.json`. Not mapped: `socket:tesla_supercharger` (deprecated on the wiki). `socket:gb_ac` maps to `plug-type-2` (ruling above).
