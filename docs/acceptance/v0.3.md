# Acceptance: v0.3

Scope: the 11 new icons of v0.3 (connectors, station, power, energy, vehicle, payment and two map markers), listed in the 0.3.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and in the icon tables of the [README](../../README.md#icons).

## Delegation

On 2026-11-02 the owner (Landolsi Digital) approved the icon list for v0.3 and delegated the design decisions to the maintainer. Icons are not approved by the owner one by one; instead:

1. The icons are drawn in four batches: the connector batch (`plug-type-1`; `plug-gbt` dropped, see below), the station batch (`charging-operator`, `destination-charging`, `report-fault`), the power batch (`charging-time`, `charging-power-level`, `charging-session`, `route-to-charger`) and the meter and marker batch (`calibrated-meter`, `marker-reserved`, `marker-queue`).
2. An independent reviewer checks each batch with screenshots (`npm run snap`) at 16/20/24 px, light and dark; markers at 24/32/40 px, outline and filled.
3. After the reviewer's findings are resolved, the maintainer approves the batch: `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date of that approval. `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

All v0.3 icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.3.0"`, and `approvedBy: null`, `approvedOn: null` until their batch is approved.
