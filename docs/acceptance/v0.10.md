# Acceptance: v0.10

Scope: the new icon `plug-type-e` and search tags in Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Polish, listed in the 0.10.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Delegation

Since 2026-11-01 the owner (Landolsi Digital) delegates the design decisions to the maintainer. The scope of v0.10 was set with the owner on 2026-11-17: of the domestic sockets, only Type E is added, because it is the only one tagged often at charging stations in OpenStreetMap (taginfo, 2026-11-17: `socket:typee` 9,497, `socket:schuko` 7,183; the other national sockets below 250 each: NEMA 5-15 232, Swiss T23 212, AS/NZS 3112 145, BS 1363 44, Italian CEI 23-16 0). External icon files offered in issue #2 were not used; `plug-type-e` is drawn from a public drawing.

## Geometry checks

Recess and holes as in `plug-schuko` (circle `R = 9`, holes at `(7.625, 12)` and `(16.375, 12)`), from Wikimedia Commons "File:Type E Socket.svg" (Aeroid, public domain): 39 mm recess, holes 19 mm apart, 5 mm earth pin 10.2 mm above the centre. The pin is a ring `r = 1.5` at `(12, 7.25)` (scaled: r 1.15 at y 7.29). Clearances: pin to outline 2.75, pin to hole 4.46.

## Search tags

Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Polish tags were written for all 85 icons with the terms used in each country's EV charging market (for example "colonnina di ricarica", "punto de recarga", "laadpaal", "stacja ładowania") and checked by the maintainer on samples from every category. `tests/tags.test.ts` checks that every icon has all seven languages, lowercase, trimmed and without duplicates; the gallery test searches one term per new language.
