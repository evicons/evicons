# Acceptance: v0.7

Scope: the redrawn lightning bolts of v0.7, in the 43 icons listed in the 0.7.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md). No icon is added or removed; the meaning, tags and element order of every icon are unchanged.

## Decision

On 2026-11-04 the owner (Landolsi Digital) rejected the bolts of 0.6.0 as too wide, and the hollow ones because they leave a white hole in the middle (most visible in a marker drawn hollow: white head, dark outline and sign). The owner chose the slim solid bolt on 2026-11-04, approved for the marker head drawn filled (`M13.25 6.25 9.25 12.5h2.5L10.75 17.75l4-6.25h-2.5Z`, 5.5 × 11.5), and asked for every lightning bolt in EVicons to follow it: slim and solid, no counter. This overrides "outline only" for bolts (design guide section 2, "Lightning bolt").

Under the delegation of 2026-11-01 the maintainer made the remaining calls: three bolt sizes (small 3 × 6.5 for cues inside other icons, keeping the footprint of the old 4 × 6.5 block; the approved sign size; full size 9 × 18.5), each one exact path that is moved, never scaled; the stroke stays 1.5 on the bolts; the torn bolt of `power-outage` as two solid pieces with a crack; the larger `charging-station` display and the shorter `streetlight-charging` mast stub. `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is `2026-11-04` on all 43 icons; `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

## Filled and hollow markers

The bolt is the only element with a fill of its own (`fill="currentColor"`). Filled mode gets a third rule, `.marker svg > [fill] { fill: #fff; }`, so that the bolt is white like the rest of the sign; checked in `tests/visual/marker-fill.spec.tsx` (computed fill and stroke of the bolt are white, also with a `<title>`), in `site/tests/gallery.spec.ts` and, for map images, in `site/tests/marker-image.test.tsx` (`fill="#fff"` instead of `fill="currentColor"`). Hollow mode (pin filled white, colour on the marker) needs no further rule: the bolt stays solid in the marker colour (`tests/visual/marker-fill.spec.tsx`).
