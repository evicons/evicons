# Acceptance: v0.4

Scope: the 17 new icons of v0.4 (connectors, stations, payment and vehicles), to be listed in the 0.4.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) and in the icon tables of the [README](../../README.md#icons).

## Delegation

On 2026-11-03 the owner (Landolsi Digital) approved the icon list for v0.4 and delegated the design decisions to the maintainer. Icons are not approved by the owner one by one; instead:

1. The icons are drawn in five batches: the connector batch (`plug-ccs1`, `plug-nacs`), station batch A (`fuel-station-charging`, `dealer-charging`, `shop-charging`, `streetlight-charging`), station batch B (`charging-hub`, `highway-charging`, `accessible-charging`), the payment batch (`free-charging`, `idle-fee`, `qr-charging`, `plug-and-charge`) and the vehicle batch (`truck-electric`, `bus-electric`, `bike-electric`, `plug-in-hybrid`).
2. An independent reviewer checks each batch with screenshots (`npm run snap`) at 16/20/24 px, light and dark.
3. After the reviewer's findings are resolved, the maintainer approves the batch: `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date of that approval. `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

All v0.4 icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.4.0"`, and `approvedBy: null`, `approvedOn: null` until their batch is approved.
