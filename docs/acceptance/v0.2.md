# Acceptance: v0.2

Scope: the 29 new icons of v0.2 (connectors, station, energy, vehicle, power, payment and the eight map markers), see `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-evicons-v0.2-design.md` section 5.

## Delegation

On 2026-11-01 the owner (Landolsi Digital) delegated the design decisions for v0.2 to the maintainer. Icons are not approved by the owner one by one; instead:

1. The icons are drawn in batches (connectors, station, vehicle and power, payment, markers).
2. An independent reviewer checks each batch with screenshots (`npm run snap`) at 16/20/24 px, light and dark; markers at 24/32/40 px, outline and filled.
3. After the reviewer's findings are resolved, the maintainer approves the batch: `provenance.approvedBy` is `"Landolsi Digital"` and `approvedOn` is the date of that approval. `approvedBy` holds the company name, never a person.

All v0.2 icons are `createdBy: "ai"`, `since: "0.2.0"`, and `approvedBy: null`, `approvedOn: null` until their batch is approved.
