## Geometry checks

`motorcycle-electric`: wheels `r = 2.75` at `(5.5, 18.25)` and `(18.5, 18.25)`; seat `<rect width="8.25" height="3" x="2.5" y="10.25" rx="1.5"/>`; body `M10.75 12.5v1.25a1.5 1.5 0 0 0 1.5 1.5h5.6` (down from the seat to the footboard, which ends on the steering column); handlebar and column `M14.25 5.5h2l2.25 12.75` (to the front hub); small bolt at origin (5.25, 1.75). Clearances between centre lines, measured on sampled outlines: bolt to seat 2.0, seat to rear wheel 2.25, body to rear wheel 3.86, seat to column 6.48. The footboard meets the column 1.18 above the front wheel: a joint at the fork, like the frame lines of `bike-electric` at the hubs, not two parallel strokes.

`power-outage`: the full bolt (9 × 18.5, `tools/lib/bolts.ts`) at origin (5, 2.75) and the cross `m15.25 15.75 4.5 4.5m0-4.5-4.5 4.5`; bolt to cross 3.5. The struck-through variant was not chosen: cut with the 2.0 gap of the crossed-out rule it leaves two tips and two slivers of the bolt, which blur at 16 px.
