# Acceptance: v0.11

Scope: five new icons, listed in the 0.11.0 section of [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## Scope decisions

The candidates were things that almost every charging app shows and for which it had to fall back on a general icon set. A sixth candidate, `status-charging`, was dropped: `status-occupied` already carries OCPI `EVSE.status=CHARGING`, and `charging-session` is the user's own live session. `status-blocked` and `status-planned` rest on OCPI (`BLOCKED`, `PLANNED`); in OpenStreetMap planned stations are rare (taginfo, 2026-11-17: `construction:amenity=charging_station` 361, `proposed:` 81, `planned:` 9), so no OSM tag is mapped.
