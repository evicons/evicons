title: Design principles
---

Every icon follows the [design guide](https://github.com/evicons/evicons/blob/main/docs/design-guide.md); the linter enforces its technical rules. In short:

- **Grid and stroke:** 24 × 24 with at least 1 unit to the edge (live area 1–23), stroke width 1.5, round caps and joins.
- **Outline only:** no fills, no colours, no opacity; colour comes from CSS through `currentColor`.
- **Soft shapes, more detail:** outer corners round (radius 1.25 or more) where the subject allows; more inner detail than Lucide, but readable at 16 px and matching Lucide's optical size at 20 px.
- **Spacing:** at least 2.5 units between the centre lines of parallel strokes; 2.0 at the very least where a subject needs it, such as connector faces.
- **Documented exceptions:** deliberate deviations are declared per icon: `letters`, `dense-connector`, `sharp-corner` (lightning bolt tips, the marker corner) and `solid-sign` (the small bolt inside other icons).
- **Letters:** AC, DC, kW, P and R come from one stroke alphabet with cap height 8 (5.25 in marker heads) and a gap of 2 units between letters; a lone letter that is the main subject may use cap height 11.
- **Connectors:** the vehicle inlet seen from the front, with the contact layout checked against a public drawing of the standard. Schuko, which has no vehicle inlet, shows the socket face.
- **Status:** one round frame (`<circle cx="12" cy="12" r="9"/>`) for every status; the sign inside identifies it without colour.
- **Markers:** one standard pin, always the first `<path>`; the sign stays inside the pin head.
- **Drawn from scratch:** no paths copied, traced or adapted from Lucide or any other icon set.

### Right-to-left layouts

EVicons are not mirrored automatically. In a right-to-left layout, mirror the icons that show a direction: `route-to-charger`, `charging-plug`, `plug-and-charge`, `charging-cable`, `car-plugged`, `bidirectional-charging` and all vehicles (`car-electric`, `truck-electric`, `bus-electric`, `bike-electric`, `motorcycle-electric`). Never mirror the letter icons, the connector faces (category `connector`: `plug-type-1`, `plug-type-2`, `plug-ccs1`, `plug-ccs2`, `plug-chademo`, `plug-nacs`, `plug-gbt-dc`, `plug-mcs`, `plug-schuko`, `plug-cee-blue`, `plug-cee-red`), `status-out-of-service` or the map markers.

```css
[dir="rtl"] .evicon-mirror { transform: scaleX(-1); }
```

Then add the class to the icons that should flip: `<CarElectric className="evicon-mirror" />`.
